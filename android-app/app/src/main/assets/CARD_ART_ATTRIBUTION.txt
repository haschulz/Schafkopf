The vector card artwork in schafkopf.android.CardArt is an original adaptation
inspired by the suit colors, double-ended court-card layout, and ornamental
framing shown in the reference. The reference photograph itself is not included
in the application. Changes include new vector suit signs, mirrored court
figures, a custom card back, and removal of the sixes to match the game's
32-card deck. The adapted card artwork is made available under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Wikimedia marks each symbol public domain in its country of origin and
countries applying a life-plus-70-years-or-less copyright term. The SVGs are
rasterized as transparent PNG resources in drawable-nodpi. These symbols replace
the suit indices in both corners and the inner pips on number cards. The SVG
source code itself is not included in the app.
